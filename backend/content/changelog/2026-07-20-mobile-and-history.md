# A phone-first pass over the whole app

You film on a phone, so you read your results on a phone. This release tightened
that path end to end:

- **One-row tab bar** at the bottom instead of a cramped wrapped nav.
- Saved-analysis cards are denser, so more of your history fits on screen.
- A **back-to-Home** button on every sub-view, so you're never stuck.
- **Clear all** in History now asks first — it used to wipe your history on a single mis-tap.

Plus a new Academy article on trunk lean (run tall, not folded), and the coach's
knee verdict is now grounded in phase-specific optima instead of one average
target for the whole stride.
