# The band says what it grades

A runner wrote in: "Elbow 57–100°, optimal 80–95°, and you say in range?"

Fair. The band was right and the page was not. Your elbow swings thirty or
forty degrees every stride — back at the drive, forward at the recovery —
and the reference is about the **carry**, the angle the arm sits around on
average. The status was graded on that mean all along. But the table put
"Optimal" and "Status" beside the Low and High columns, which invited the
comparison it was never about.

**Now every band says what it is a statement about.** In the joint table
the Optimal and Status columns are labelled *mean*, and the verdict reads
"mean in range". Under the video, a curve whose band is about the mean is
drawn plain with a dashed mean line in the verdict's colour — the swing
around it is the movement, not a fault. The bike knee at the bottom and the
hip at the top keep their coloured dots at those moments, as before. Nothing
changed in what is measured or how it is graded; what changed is that the
page no longer asks you to compare a swing with a carry.
