# Aero & watts: what your position actually costs you

Cycling analyses now open with an **Aero & watts** panel. Instead of just telling
you your trunk angle, it translates your position into the thing you care about:
watts of drag, and the time that costs you over a distance.

- A **watt range** rather than a single fake-precise number — the estimate comes from your trunk angle, so it deserves a range.
- A **multi-lever breakdown**: torso, head tuck and elbow position scored separately, so you know which one to work on first.
- Numbers personalised to **your speed**, framed as time saved.

This is a *relative* read, not a wind-tunnel measurement — it tells you whether a
change helped, not your absolute CdA.
