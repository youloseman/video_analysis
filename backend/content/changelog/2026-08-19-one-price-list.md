# One price list, and a Full plan that keeps its promise

Prices lived in four documents: the pricing screen, the billing toggle that
rewrote the same three numbers, the landing page, and the Terms of Service.
They drifted, and the drift landed somewhere it shouldn't have — the pricing
footer said the amounts were in US dollars while the Terms said Canadian ones.
That is a 37% gap between two documents you read before paying, and one of them
is the agreement.

**There is now one price list, and every page reads from it.** The Terms table,
the landing page and the pricing screen are all rendered from the same
definition, so a price can no longer be updated in three places out of four.
Everything is in **USD**, said the same way everywhere, and the Terms now
explain what that means if your card is issued in another currency.

**The Full plan now actually includes the Expert Review it advertises.** It said
"1 Expert Review included" while nothing in the product could grant one or let
you claim it — you'd have had to email and ask, and nothing on our side would
have agreed with you. The review is added to your account when you pay, the
pricing screen offers to spend it instead of selling you another, and an unused
one stays yours if you cancel: you paid for the term that included it.

At the same time, Full stopped claiming three things that don't exist yet
(multi-bike profiles, a branded PDF, cross-profile comparison). They're being
built, and each bullet comes back the day it's true rather than the day we
hope it will be.

A few smaller corrections in the same pass:

- **Expert Review is a card now, not a footnote.** It was a thin strip under
  the plans, which is a strange place for the only part of Flapp with a human
  behind it.
- **The free plan admits to the cloud history it was already giving you.** Sync
  across devices has always worked on free accounts; the plan card said it
  didn't.
- **Prices updated:** Enthusiast is $9/month or $69/year, Full is $99/year, and
  an Expert Review is $39.
