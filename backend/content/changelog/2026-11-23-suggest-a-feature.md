# Suggest a feature

At the bottom of What's new there is now a box for ideas: *a rear view for running*, *two bikes side by side*, whatever you wish Flapp did.

- **One reader.** Every idea goes to the triathlete who builds Flapp, not to a public board.
- **We write back when it ships.** If your idea is built, you get an email that says so. Releases that riders asked for are marked *Asked for by riders* in this list.
