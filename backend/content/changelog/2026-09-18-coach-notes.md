# Coach Notes — three things a coach sees, $12

Between the AI coach's notes and the $39 Expert Review there was nothing.
Plenty of people want a person to look at their clip; fewer want the full
review. Now there is a rung between.

**Coach Notes.** At the bottom of any report: a coach reads the analysis and
your clip and writes back **the first thing to change**, in one line, and
**three notes** — what a person sees that the numbers didn't. Written onto
the report itself, usually within 72 hours: they sit under "Coach Notes",
print with the report and go into the AI export as their own section, marked
as written by a person. $12, once, on any plan.

What it is not, said before you pay: a review. No judgement on which numbers
to trust, no four-week plan, no position trade-offs with their costs. Those
are the Expert Review's, and the card says so.

The format is fixed on purpose — one line and three notes of about forty
words each, enforced in the editor and on the server. That is what keeps the
$12 product a $12 product and the $39 one worth $39, and it is what makes a
coach's twelve minutes honest ones.

Supply is one person's time, so like the Expert Review it has a weekly cap
and a pause switch; the card tells you before you click if this week is
booked or paused.

Also this week, quietly: a coach's own profile and note editor — built for
coaches who hand reports to athletes — are switched off until there are
coaches asking for them. Nothing is lost; it is parked.
