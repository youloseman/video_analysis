# Pictures for every reading

- **Each reading leads with its drawing:** the rider or runner, with the joint it measures marked in the reading's colour. In Findings, on Home, in What to fix, in the measurements table and in the PDF.
- **How to film it is six drawn pairs:** the way to film it next to the mistake people make most, from filling the frame to holding the phone level.
- **Light behind you, drawn:** the beams show light going onto you, or past you into the lens.
- **A new card for stories:** the full frame, your score ring, the grade in words and how far you have come since your first analysis.
