# Forearm tilt leaves the road rubric

Forearm tilt is an aero-bar measure: how much the extensions and armrests
point up, the thing the UCI regulates and the wind-tunnel papers describe.
It was also being scored on the hoods and in the drops, against a band
borrowed from the aero positions — and on the hoods your forearm slopes
*down* to the bar, because that is where the bar is.

Three clips of one rider on the hoods showed what that did. On two of them
the forearm read −42° and was quietly dropped as implausible; on the third
it read −25°, passed, and scored 5 out of 100 against a target of 5–20°.
Because the score is pulled toward its weakest component, that one measure
took the clip from a B to a D. Nothing about the rider had changed.

**Road positions — hoods, drops, casual — are now scored on eight measures,
and the coverage line says eight.** Aero positions keep the forearm as
before. The fit plan no longer lists a hoods forearm as "already in range"
either; it was praising geometry.

If a road clip of yours re-scores higher today, this is why. The angles
themselves are unchanged.
