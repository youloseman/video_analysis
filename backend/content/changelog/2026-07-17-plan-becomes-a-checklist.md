# Your training plan lives on as a checklist

A drill list you read once and forget is worthless. The corrective drills from
your analysis now follow you: they land in your dashboard as a **checklist you
tick off**, so the plan survives past the page you read it on.

Also in this release:

- **Pedal-phase picker on photo analysis.** Hip and ankle angles mean completely different things at the top and bottom of the pedal stroke. If we can't tell which one your photo caught, you now tell us — and the score follows.
- Joints whose verdict depends on pedal phase are **no longer judged blind**.
- History loads faster: annotated frames are fetched as cards scroll into view instead of all at once.
