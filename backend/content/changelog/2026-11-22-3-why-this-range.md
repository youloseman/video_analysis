# Why this range

A coach asked us why our ranges are the "optimal" ones. Good question, and the report now answers it for every reading.

- **Tap the ? beside a range** and a line opens under the reading: why the range is what it is, in two or three sentences.
- **What kind of evidence it is**, said plainly: *measured in studies*, *bike-fit guidance* or *what the pros ride*. Not every range has a study behind it, and the ones that don't say so.
- **Links to the studies themselves.** Every link was opened and checked.
- **In the PDF too:** *Where the ranges come from* sits under the table, with the links.
