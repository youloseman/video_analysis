# The report on a phone

Most reports are read on a phone, and on a phone the report was hard work.

- **One column.** The findings used to squeeze in beside the clip and the page scrolled sideways. They now sit under the video, full width.
- **The score in two lines.** What it is, then the score with its grade beside it — the findings start a screen higher.
- **Less chrome under the clip.** The player's controls went from five rows to three.
- **Tiles that fit their text.** A metric tile is no longer as tall as the tallest one beside it.
