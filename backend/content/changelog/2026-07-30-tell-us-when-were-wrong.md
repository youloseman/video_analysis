# Tell us when the analysis is wrong

You can't verify a pose estimate or a 0–100 score by looking at it — you can only
tell whether it matches the athlete you know yourself to be. That verdict is the
one thing we can't measure for ourselves, so we're now asking for it.

Every report ends with **"Does this match what you see?"**

- **Looks right** is one click and you're done.
- **Something's off** opens the reasons — didn't track me, angles look wrong, score too low or too high, coaching too generic, overlay broken — plus a box to say it in your own words.
- Optionally, attach the **annotated still** from that analysis. It's a photo of you, so it is never sent unless you tick the box. It is also the only way to see what the analyzer saw when it got you wrong.

Every rating arrives with the analyzer's own read of that clip — how well it
tracked you, how confident it was, which quality warnings fired. That's what
turns "the score felt low" into a fixable bug: we can see whether the complaints
cluster on clips the analyzer already knew were marginal, or on clips it thought
were perfect.

We read all of it. We don't reply to every message, but it decides what gets
fixed next — and you'll see the result here.
