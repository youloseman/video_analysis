# Your first report is the real one

The free tier showed a score and nothing else. That proves the thing runs; it
does not tell you whether what's behind the blur is worth paying for, because
you have never seen it. You were being asked to buy on trust.

**Your first analysis now comes back as a real report.** The findings — what we
measured and what fired — and the coach's notes written about them, in full,
with the numbers left on the frame. No card, no trial, nothing to cancel.

Two sections stay behind the blur: the **joint-angle table** and the
**corrective drills**. Those are the ongoing product rather than the sample —
the plan is what changes the *next* report, and one report has no next. The
upgrade prompt now says exactly what it is holding back, counted: "3 corrective
drills, all 7 joint angles we measured." Vague felt like a strange thing to
follow a full report with.

After that, free analyses go back to the score-and-skeleton preview, three a
month as before.

Some details, because the edges matter:

- **A failed analysis doesn't burn it.** Your first-ever clip is the one most
  likely to be filmed from a bad angle, and it would have been the worst
  possible one to spend this on. If the run fails, the report is still waiting
  for you.
- **Existing accounts get one too.** You signed up when the free tier was a
  score and a skeleton; it seemed fair to show you what we've been building
  rather than to quietly grandfather you out of it.
- The annotated overlay video isn't part of it — that stays a paid feature.
