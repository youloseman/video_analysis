# Your clips now stay for a year on a paid plan

Until today a paid plan kept your uploaded clips for 30 days (60 on the Coach
plan). That was long enough for a review, but not long enough for the question
people actually ask a history: *what did I look like last season?*

**From 11 November, every paid plan keeps clips for 12 months**, the Coach plan
included. Last spring's TT position can now play next to this autumn's, and a
CdA you measure months after filming still has the clip it belongs to.

- **The free plan is unchanged:** 7 days.
- **Clips uploaded before 11 November** keep the period they were uploaded
  under. We don't keep footage longer than we said we would when you sent it.
- **Deleting still means deleting.** Remove an analysis or your account and the
  clip goes with it, straight away.
- **If a paid plan ends,** your clips stay for 30 more days. We email you when
  it ends and again 5 days before they go, so a failed card never costs you a
  season of footage overnight. After that the free plan's 7 days apply.

The numbers, frames and notes in your saved analyses were always kept for good;
this is about the video behind them.
