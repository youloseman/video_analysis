# Worth a look: every reading outside its range, with what to check

A coach's report once said "Nothing to fix" directly under a table with two
red rows. The numbers were right; the summary was wrong, and somebody who came
looking for room to improve was told there was none.

That cannot happen any more. When a measurement is clearly outside its range,
the coach's notes have to deal with it — "Nothing to fix" is reserved for a
position where nothing is.

And every report now ends with **Worth a look**: each reading outside its
range or on its edge, listed with what usually causes it and what to check or
try first. Built from the same verdicts as your joint table, not written by
the AI, so nothing on the table is left out of it. For example:

- **Knee at the top of the stroke, more open than the range:** usually short cranks or a saddle on the high side. If the knee at the bottom also sits at the top of its range, try the saddle 3–5 mm lower first; on cranks of 165 mm or shorter the reading is expected.
- **Helmet high above the back:** dropping the head a little cuts drag, as long as you can still see the road.

Where the honest answer is "often deliberate" or "re-film before changing
anything", it says that. A hint is a starting point, not a diagnosis: these
are 2D pose measurements, good to a few degrees.
