# Slow-motion clips no longer produce wrong numbers

If you filmed in slow motion, your phone's frame rate lies about time — and
anything time-based (cadence, ground contact, flight time) came out wrong
without saying so.

Now the analyzer detects it. When the time base can't be trusted, those metrics
are **withheld and explained** rather than shown wrong, and the report tells you
plainly that a normal-speed clip is needed for them. Your joint angles are
unaffected — they don't depend on the clock.

A missing number you can see is worth more than a confident number that's wrong.
