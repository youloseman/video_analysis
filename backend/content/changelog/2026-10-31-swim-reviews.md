# New: your swim stroke, reviewed by a pro

Flapp has been run and bike only. Now you can send a swim clip too.

Film yourself in the pool and upload it under **Analyze → Swim · video**. A pro triathlete who was a swimmer first watches it and writes back what to change: body line, head and breathing, the catch, the kick, the rhythm.

- **Coach Notes, $12:** the first thing to change and three short notes, within 72 hours.
- **Expert Review, $39:** what to fix in what order, drills for each, and what to film to check it worked, within 5 days. Full and Enthusiast members pay their member price, and a review included in a yearly Full plan covers a swim clip too.

How to film it: a camera underwater, 30–60 cm deep, side-on to your lane, 3–5 m away. Two or three lengths at your normal pace, so both breathing sides are in it. Ask the pool first: many don't allow filming, or need the staff's OK. Film only your own lane.

There is no automatic analysis for swimming yet — no score and no angles. The pose models we use for running and cycling can't read a body underwater, so for now a person watches every clip. Nothing is charged at upload: you choose Notes or a review on the next screen.
