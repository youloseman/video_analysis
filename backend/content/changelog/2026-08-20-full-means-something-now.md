# The Full plan means something now

For a while the Full card promised three things that did not exist: profiles
for more than one bike, a branded PDF, and an Expert Review included with the
plan. We took those claims off rather than leave them there. They are back,
because they are now true.

**Equipment profiles.** Say which bike — or which pair of shoes — a clip was
filmed on, right next to the clip. A road position and a TT position are both
correct and differ on purpose, so averaging them into one history produces a
trend line describing nobody.

**Compare two setups, not just two weeks.** Each setup averaged across
everything filmed on it, side by side. This is the part a single bought report
can never be: one setup cannot be compared with anything.

**A report you can hand to a coach.** Print or save as PDF, with the upgrade
panels and the buttons taken out and a proper header put in — a document rather
than a screenshot of a web page.

**Your included Expert Review renews.** It arrives when you subscribe and again
each year. Previously the second year would have quietly arrived without it.

Two things that apply to everyone:

- **The free plan keeps one equipment profile**, and Enthusiast does too.
  Keeping several is what makes the comparison possible, so that is where the
  line sits — rather than in an analysis quota nobody reaches.
- **Expert Reviews are limited to a handful a week.** Each one is about forty
  minutes of a real person's attention, and selling more of them than that
  person can write would turn a premium thing into a queue and an apology.
  If a week is full, you will be told before you pay, not after.
