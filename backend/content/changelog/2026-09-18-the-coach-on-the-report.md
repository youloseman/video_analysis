# The coach on the report

Full is the plan coaches are on, and the report they handed an athlete said
Flapp at the top and nothing about who read it. Every note the coach wanted
to add lived in a message beside the link.

**Your coach profile** — under Account, on the Full plan. A name, a line
under it, a mark. From then on every report you print carries your mark and
your name where the brand sat, and "Analysed with Flapp" moves to the
footer, where an instrument belongs.

**Your notes on a report.** Under the AI coach's notes there is now a block
that is yours: what you saw that the numbers did not, and the one thing to
work on before the next clip. It is saved on the report, opens with it from
History, prints under your name, and travels into the AI export as its own
section — marked as written by a person, so a model reading the file defers
to it.

**A cue library.** The phrases you say every week — "land under your hips",
"quiet upper body" — kept once on your profile and offered as one-click chips
when you write a note. The forum behind this feature had a thread asking for
exactly that in 2011; it was never built.

Athletes on the same account who open the report see the note read-only,
under the coach's name. Everything else on the page is unchanged: the note
is beside the analysis, never inside it.
