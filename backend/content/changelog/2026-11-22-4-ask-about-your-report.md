# Ask about your report

Under every report there is now a box for your questions: *how do I fix my elbow?*, *is this good for long rides?*, *how do I film a better clip?* The answers come from that report's numbers, your bike setup and any CdA you have logged, not from the internet.

- **Tap a question or type your own.** The suggested ones are written from your report, so the first one is usually about the reading furthest out of its range.
- **On Home and on Progress too.** Home asks about your latest ride or run; Progress asks about the clips on the page, so *why did my score go up?* gets an answer from your own series.
- **In your language.** Ask in Russian, Spanish or anything else and the answer comes back in it.
- **It remembers what you tell it**, like a race in June or a stem you cannot change, so it does not ask again. *What it remembers* shows the notes; *Forget all of it* clears them.
- **It knows its limits.** It does not guess a CdA from video, does not name parts it cannot confirm fit your frame, and for pain it sends you to a fitter or a physio.

Included on Enthusiast (50 questions a month), Full (200) and Coach (500).
