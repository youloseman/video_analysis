# My technique, as a checklist

- **What to fix is a list you can tick.** Each item names the reading, its range and the change: **↓ Saddle 5 mm**, **→ Stem +10 mm**. Tick it when you have made the change; the report you made it from ticks too.
- **The sport with something to change comes first.** It never says *Nothing to fix* over a reading that is out of range.
- **One sport at a time on a phone**, with Run and Bike as tabs.
- **Progress on a phone** is one row per reading that opens its full chart, and readings filmed differently are never drawn on one line.
- **Compare shows every measurement on its range**, before and after, so what moved is visible at a glance.
