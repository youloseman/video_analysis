# A simpler report

We went through every screen with one rule: a picture should replace words, not add to them.

- **Fix first is one line and one action.** The reading against its range, then the change as a chip: **→ Stem +10 mm**, **↑ Cadence +5%**.
- **Confidence is three bars and two words.** The detail opens under *Why*.
- **The chat asks short questions**, the way you would tap them.
- **Uploading has one decision less.** The camera setup is one line, already set for most clips; tap *change* if you filmed both sides.
- **Less repeated text.** No second copy of the same advice, no allowance line on every screen, and *Compare* is one button.
- **A photo report says what a photo is** in one line, with the way to a video right under it.
