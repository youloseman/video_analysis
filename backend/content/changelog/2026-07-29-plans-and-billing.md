# Plans, quotas and a free tier that's honest about it

Flapp now has paid plans, and a free tier that shows you exactly what it is
rather than pretending to be the full product.

- **Starter (free)** — 3 analyses a month: your score, your grade and an annotated frame.
- **Enthusiast** — 30 analyses a month, with the measurements, AI coaching, training plan and the annotated overlay video unlocked.
- **Full** — 120 analyses a month.

The free tier is deliberately a *teaser*, not a crippled version: you get the
skeleton and the score, and the report tells you plainly which sections a paid
plan unlocks instead of showing you empty panels. Checkout and plan management
run through Stripe, and you can cancel from your own billing portal at any time.
