# Unlock one report for $4

Flapp is used episodically. You change your saddle height, or your shoes, or
come back from an injury — you film, you want that one answer, and you are gone
for three months. A subscription is priced for a habit you don't have, so the
honest read is that most people were never going to take one, and were being
offered nothing else.

**Any report you can't read now has a $4 button on it.** That opens the joint
angles and the corrective drills for that one analysis. No plan, no card kept,
nothing to cancel.

What it doesn't open is everything that needs a *second* analysis to mean
anything — trends, before / after, the overlay video. That isn't a paywall
being coy: one report genuinely cannot be compared with anything. Comparison is
what a plan is for, and we'd rather say so than pretend the two products are
the same one at different prices.

Two other things changed with it:

- **Ten free analyses a month, up from three.** Uploading was never the
  expensive part — a locked report runs no AI call and renders no video — and
  the second clip is the one you film *after changing something*, which is the
  interesting one. Reading is what costs money now, so the cap is just there to
  stop abuse.
- **Subscribing opens everything you ever ran**, including reports you looked
  at through the blur months ago. If you've bought a couple of unlocks and then
  take a plan, you are not starting again.

And if you unlock two reports, we'll tell you plainly that a month costs about
the same and comes with more — rather than letting you work that out on the
third one and feel taken.
