# Road ranges from measured riders

We checked every road range against what studies actually measured on riders, and moved the ones that did not match.

- **On the hoods:** trunk 32–50°, shoulder 70–90°, elbow 145–170°. Trained riders measured 37–42° at the trunk and 76–78° at the shoulder; the old ranges marked most racers down for a normal position.
- **In the drops:** trunk 22–38°, 8–10° under the hoods, as measured on the same riders.
- **The drops elbow and shoulder are shown, not graded.** No study has measured them, so we no longer pretend to know the right number.
- **A low road trunk is a racing position, not a fault.** The report no longer tells you to raise the bars for it.
- **Knee and hip ranges are unchanged** for now.

Most road scores move up a point or two. Reports you already have keep the ranges they were graded on.
