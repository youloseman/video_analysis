# The caveat now comes with the score

A number with no caveat reads as a fact. That was the problem with the free
report: it showed a score and nothing else — no angles, no coaching, nothing to
argue with — so if the analyzer had only half-tracked you, or you'd filmed from
an awkward angle, you had no way to know. The score looked equally confident
either way.

**Free reports now carry the analyzer's own verdict on the footage.** If the
quality gate tripped, you see why. If tracking was poor, if more than one person
was in frame, if the clip looked like slow motion — you see that too, along with
the confidence tier. None of it is behind the paywall, because it isn't a
feature: it's the difference between a measurement and a guess.

**Long clips say so.** Past a certain length we sample every second, third or
fourth frame to keep the analysis tractable. Joint angles don't care, but cadence,
ground contact and flight time are counted in frames — so they get coarser. The
app now warns you when you pick a long clip, and the report repeats it with the
actual numbers. **5–10 s** is still the sweet spot.

**Your results are yours.** A job link used to be readable by anyone who had it.
Results are now tied to the browser that uploaded them — or to your account, if
you're signed in — so a pasted link no longer hands over your footage.

**You can delete your account yourself.** Dashboard → Delete account, confirmed
with your password, erases the account, every saved analysis and its stills, your
usage history, any feedback you sent, and your order history. Previously this
meant emailing us and waiting.
