# Your clip sticks around now

Until today, footage was deleted six hours after the analysis. That rule was
written when a clip existed only long enough to be measured, and it quietly
undermined the one thing on the pricing page with a human behind it: an Expert
Review bought on Tuesday for Monday's ride had nothing left to watch. The
report template literally opened by apologising for it.

**Clips are now kept for 7 days on the free plan and 30 days on a paid one**,
and a clip with an Expert Review that has been paid for and not yet delivered
is kept until the review ships, however long that takes. Your reviewer watches
the movement now, not one frame of it.

Two things had to be fixed to make that work, and only one of them was storage.
A saved analysis and the upload it came from were **unrelated objects** — your
history entry had no idea which file produced it — so even footage that had
survived would have been unfindable. Analyses record their upload from now on.

**Deleting still means deleting.** Because clips now live for weeks, "delete
this analysis" had to start removing the footage too, not just the record of
it. Deleting a single analysis, clearing your history, or deleting your account
all take the video with them, immediately. The privacy policy has been rewritten
with the real numbers rather than "within hours".

One honest limit: analyses from before today have no link to their footage, and
that footage is long gone anyway. Reviews bought against them still work the way
they always did — from the stored analysis and its annotated frame.
