# A share card actually worth posting

The share card was a wall of metric tiles that nobody would post to Strava. It's
now built around the one thing that carries: **your annotated photo**, with the
score ring sitting on the seam and your coach's one-line takeaway underneath.

- The photo is the hero, cropped **by its actual content** rather than a fixed guess, so you don't lose your head to the frame edge.
- Metric tiles are gone; the score and the takeaway carry the card.
- Generated entirely in your browser — the image never touches our server.

Also: the app shell is now served with revalidation headers, so a new release
reaches you on the next load instead of whenever your browser feels like it.
